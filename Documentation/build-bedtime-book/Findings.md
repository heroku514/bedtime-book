# Findings - Bedtime Book

## 2026-10-03

- Readlight is a warm lamp with a sleep timer. Pupila, Luna Story, Moonlit AI, and LumiNap are bedtime stories, often with a subscription. None of them require the lamp to stay on until the book shuts.
- Shipped rules already cover a one-use match, a three-plank bridge, a two-hand bow, a coat button order, and a two-seat blanket. This app does not reuse those.
- The unfinished jam-jar lid is a different mechanic and is not continued.
