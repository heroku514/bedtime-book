# Plan - Bedtime Book

## Goal

Ship one free offline iOS app for kids, family, or seniors. The lamp must be on before the book opens, and the lamp stays on until the book shuts.

## Approach

1. Confirm no App Store rejection is waiting.
2. Build a one-screen Expo app with that rule and on-device storage.
3. Pass a full simulator regression on the Release binary, then take three screenshots.
4. Upload that source with fastlane and submit the listing for review.
