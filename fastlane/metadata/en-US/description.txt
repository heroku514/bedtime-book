Bedtime Book keeps one pretend lamp and one book on the phone. The room starts dark. Turn the lamp on, then open the book. The lamp stays on while the book is open. Shut the book before the lamp can go off. The huge line says the room is dark, the light is on, or it is time to read.

The book stays on this phone. Start a new book when you want the room dark again.

Bedtime Book does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. It does not light a real room. The lamp and the book in the app are original.
